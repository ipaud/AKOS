# Product Score (0–100)

Measures product clarity — real problem, defined outcome, validated before building. Advisory (the human owns product decisions, [Ruling R10](../core/conflict-resolution.md)).

## Inputs

- [inspired](../packs/product/inspired/scoring-rubric.md) — four risks
- [lean-startup](../packs/product/lean-startup/scoring-rubric.md) — MVP/validated learning
- [escaping-the-build-trap](../packs/product/escaping-the-build-trap/scoring-rubric.md) — outcomes over output
- [continuous-discovery-habits](../packs/product/continuous-discovery-habits/scoring-rubric.md)

## Deductions

- No outcome statement / value risk never tested / no discovery before multi-week build: −15 (HIGH)
- Strategy-as-backlog, vanity-metric-only success measure: −10-15 (HIGH)
- Engineers absent from discovery, no post-launch measurement, MVP scope creep: −4-6 (MEDIUM)
- Prototype over-built for the risk tested: −2 (LOW)

## Interpretation

- **90** — outcome-driven, all four risks addressed, validated cheaply.
- **75** — solid with a discovery gap.
- **60** — feature-factory tendencies, thin validation.
- **<50** — no discovery, no outcomes, ship-and-hope.

Scaled hard by profile: a genuine prototype exploring an idea isn't held to full discovery rigor. Startup MVP weights this highest (PMF search).
