# Pack: Inspired — Product Discovery & Team Practice

**Domain:** Product · **Authority:** Level 3 (methodology) · **Version:** 1.0.0

Operationalizes Marty Cagan's product-team model: empowered teams solving problems (not building roadmap features), continuous discovery to de-risk value/usability/feasibility/business viability before building, and the product manager's role as customer+business expert, not backlog administrator.

Independent distillation; not affiliated with or endorsed by the source author. See [references.md](references.md).

## When to load

- Evaluating whether a team is empowered (given problems) or a feature factory (given a roadmap of outputs).
- Structuring product discovery before committing engineering time.
- Reviewing whether a PRD/spec addresses the four risks (value, usability, feasibility, viability).
