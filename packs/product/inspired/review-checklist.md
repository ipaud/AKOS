# Review Checklist — Inspired Pack

## High

- [ ] Initiative has a written outcome statement (metric + target), not just a feature description. (IE1)
- [ ] All four risks (value, usability, feasibility, viability) addressed in discovery notes, even briefly. (IE2)
- [ ] A prototype or equivalent low-fidelity validation preceded production build on non-trivial initiatives. (IE3)
- [ ] Engineers were involved in discovery before the solution was locked, not only at spec handoff. (IE4)

## Medium

- [ ] Post-launch outcome measurement is planned/scheduled, not left implicit. (IE5)
- [ ] The team's backlog reflects problems/outcomes, not only a list of pre-decided output features.
- [ ] Discovery has a real kill option — evidence that not everything proposed ships.

## Low

- [ ] Prototype fidelity matched the risk being tested (not over-built for the question asked).
- [ ] Vision/strategy/roadmap distinctions are clear in planning docs, not conflated.
