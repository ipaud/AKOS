# Glossary — Inspired Pack

- **Empowered team** — a team given a problem/outcome to solve, with latitude over the solution.
- **Feature team** — a team given a pre-decided list of outputs to build on schedule.
- **Discovery** — the pre-build process of validating an idea against the four risks, cheaply and quickly.
- **Four risks** — value, usability, feasibility, business viability.
- **Outcome** — a change in customer/business behavior an initiative is meant to cause; contrasted with output.
- **Prototype** — a low-fidelity artifact built to answer a specific discovery question, not for production use.
- **Product vision** — the multi-year direction a product is heading.
- **Product strategy** — the sequence of problems chosen to pursue the vision.
- **Concierge / Wizard-of-Oz test** — a discovery technique delivering a service manually to test demand before automating it.
