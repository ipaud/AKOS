# Engineering Rules — Inspired Pack

Applied at the process/artifact level (this pack governs product process, not code).

- IE1. Every initiative entering a build phase has a written outcome statement (a metric expected to move, with a target) — not only a feature description.
- IE2. Every initiative's discovery record addresses value, usability, feasibility, and viability explicitly, even if briefly — blank sections are flagged.
- IE3. A prototype or equivalent low-fidelity validation exists before production engineering work begins on any non-trivial (multi-week) initiative.
- IE4. Engineers are named participants in discovery for initiatives they'll build, documented in discovery notes/meeting attendance — not first exposed to the idea at spec handoff.
- IE5. Post-launch, the stated outcome metric is measured and reported — initiatives without a post-launch outcome check are flagged as incomplete regardless of ship status.
