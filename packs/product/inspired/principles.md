# Principles — Inspired Pack

- **IN1 — Give teams problems, not features.** A team's charter is a business outcome or customer problem to solve, not a pre-decided solution to build.
- **IN2 — Validate before you build.** Every non-trivial idea passes through discovery (fast, cheap validation) before committing engineering build time.
- **IN3 — Address all four risks explicitly**: value, usability, feasibility, business viability. A plan silent on one of these has an unmanaged risk, not a solved one.
- **IN4 — Prototype at the lowest fidelity that answers the question.** Don't build production code to learn something a clickable mockup or a spike could answer this week.
- **IN5 — Product, design, and engineering collaborate continuously**, not in a handoff relay (spec → build). Engineers contribute feasibility insight during discovery, not after commitments are made.
- **IN6 — Measure outcomes, not output volume.** "We shipped 40 features" is not a success metric; "retention moved from X to Y" is.
- **IN7 — Kill ideas fast and cheaply, or the market will kill them slowly and expensively.** A discovery process that never says "no, don't build this" isn't actually doing discovery.
- **IN8 — The PM owns deep customer and data knowledge**, not just requirement-gathering and prioritization logistics.
