# Pack: Escaping the Build Trap

**Domain:** Product · **Authority:** Level 3 (methodology) · **Version:** 1.0.0

Operationalizes Melissa Perri's diagnosis and cure for the "build trap": organizations that measure success by shipping velocity instead of value delivered, and the product-led organizational structures (strategy, product kata, org design) that get them out.

Independent distillation; not affiliated with or endorsed by the source author. See [references.md](references.md).

## When to load

- Diagnosing organizational feature-factory symptoms (not just team-level, but structural: incentives, roadmaps, exec reporting).
- Setting product strategy that cascades from company vision to team-level problems without becoming a feature list.
- Reviewing whether a roadmap is a strategy or a disguised backlog.
