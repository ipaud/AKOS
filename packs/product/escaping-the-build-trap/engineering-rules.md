# Engineering Rules — Escaping the Build Trap Pack

Process/artifact rules (this pack governs org and product process).

- BE1. Strategy documents contain a diagnosed problem statement and a guiding policy; they do not enumerate a feature backlog as their primary content.
- BE2. Roadmaps presented to stakeholders use outcome/theme framing with rough time horizons (Now/Next/Later) rather than committed features tied to specific dates more than one planning cycle out.
- BE3. Each team-level initiative traces to a stated company/product-line outcome via an explicit, written line of reasoning (not asserted from memory).
- BE4. Leadership-level reporting includes outcome/value metrics (not exclusively output/velocity metrics) as the primary success indicators reviewed in business reviews.
- BE5. A record exists of features/requests explicitly declined with a stated strategic reason, reviewed periodically — evidence that "no" is a live option, not just output-shipping.
