# Glossary — Escaping the Build Trap Pack

- **Build trap** — an organization measuring success by output shipped instead of value delivered.
- **Guiding policy** — the strategic approach chosen to address a diagnosed problem (Rumelt's strategy kernel, adopted by Perri).
- **Product kata** — the Toyota-Kata-derived loop: direction → current condition → target condition → iterate.
- **Target condition** — a specific, time-bound outcome a team works toward, not a feature.
- **Strategy cascade / strategy deployment** — passing intent (not solutions) down through organizational layers.
- **Outcome-based roadmap** — a roadmap organized by outcomes/themes with rough horizons (Now/Next/Later) instead of committed feature-dates.
- **Value exchange system** — the mutual flow of value between company and customer that a healthy product org optimizes.
- **Good PM / bad PM** — Horowitz's distinction between a PM who determines what to build from deep problem understanding vs. one who executes a handed-down feature list.
