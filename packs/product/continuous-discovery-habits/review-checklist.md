# Review Checklist — Continuous Discovery Habits Pack

## High

- [ ] At least one customer touchpoint occurred this week; gaps tracked, not silent. (CE1)
- [ ] Opportunity solution tree exists and was updated within days of the latest research. (CE2)
- [ ] Interview questions elicit stories about specific past events, not hypotheticals/opinions. (CE3)
- [ ] Assumption map exists for any solution nearing a build commitment, with the top-priority (important + unevidenced) assumption identified. (CE4)

## Medium

- [ ] The tree shows genuine breadth: ≥2 opportunities considered, ≥2 solutions per pursued opportunity. (CD4)
- [ ] At least the cheapest viable test was run against the top-priority assumption before full build. (CE5)
- [ ] Interview participation/synthesis includes people beyond a single research function. (CE6)

## Low

- [ ] Synthesis lag between interview and tree update is days, not weeks.
- [ ] Patterns cited as findings are backed by multiple independent interviews, not a single anecdote.
