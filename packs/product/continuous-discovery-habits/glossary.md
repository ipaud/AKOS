# Glossary — Continuous Discovery Habits Pack

- **Continuous discovery** — weekly-cadence customer contact sustained indefinitely, not a discrete research phase.
- **Opportunity solution tree** — visual structure mapping an outcome to opportunities to candidate solutions to tests.
- **Opportunity** — a customer need, pain point, or desire surfaced through research.
- **Story-based interviewing** — eliciting specific past events rather than opinions or hypothetical predictions.
- **Assumption mapping** — listing and prioritizing (by importance × evidence) the assumptions a solution depends on.
- **Desirability / usability / feasibility / viability** — the four assumption categories (shared with [Inspired](../inspired/glossary.md)).
- **Synthesis** — the ongoing process of extracting opportunities/patterns from interviews and updating the tree.
- **Weekly touch** — the minimum cadence commitment: at least one customer interaction per week.
