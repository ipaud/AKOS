# Anti-Patterns — Continuous Discovery Habits Pack

## Batch-and-forget research

One 10-user study conducted before a big initiative, findings presented once in a readout deck, never revisited or added to as the initiative evolves over the following months. Fix: CDE1, CDE2 — weekly touch, continuously updated tree.

## Opinion mining disguised as research

Interviews built around "what do you think of this mockup?" and "would you use this feature?" — collecting confident opinions that don't predict actual behavior. Fix: CD3, CDE3 — story-based questions about real past events.

## Solution-first tree (single branch, single leaf)

An "opportunity solution tree" with one opportunity and one solution — the decision was already made before research started, and the tree is theater justifying it after the fact. Fix: CD4 — visible breadth of ≥2 opportunities and ≥2 solutions before committing.

## Research-as-a-service

A dedicated research team conducts interviews and delivers summarized findings to product/design/engineering, who never watch a session or read a transcript directly — secondhand synthesis loses nuance and doesn't build team-wide customer intuition. Fix: CDE6, CD8 — cross-functional participation.

## Testing everything at once

Building a full solution and then running one big usability/beta test that conflates desirability, usability, and feasibility signals into one muddy result — unable to tell which specific assumption failed. Fix: CD5, CD6 — map and test assumptions individually, cheapest/riskiest first.

## The "we don't have time this week" spiral

Discovery deprioritized during a busy sprint "just this once," which becomes the new normal within a month, and the team quietly reverts to building from stakeholder opinions. Fix: heuristic weekly-touch check treated as non-negotiable as a recurring calendar commitment.
