# Prompt Fragments — Continuous Discovery Habits Pack

## Fragment: discovery-cadence lens

```text
Apply Continuous Discovery Habits (AKOS L3) to this team's process:
- Verify a weekly customer touchpoint is happening; flag gaps.
- Check for a visible opportunity solution tree rooted in the team's
  owned outcome, showing multiple opportunities and multiple solutions
  per pursued opportunity — not a single predetermined branch.
- Review interview questions for story-eliciting phrasing ("tell me
  about the last time...") vs. opinion/hypothetical phrasing ("would
  you...", "do you think...") — flag the latter for rewrite.
- For any solution nearing build commitment, require an assumption map
  (desirability/usability/feasibility/viability) with the top-priority
  (important + unevidenced) assumption identified and tested first.
- Check cross-functional participation in interviews/synthesis, not a
  siloed research function reporting to product.
```

## Fragment: interview-guide review

```text
Review this interview guide: flag any question asking for an opinion or
a hypothetical prediction rather than a specific past story. Rewrite
flagged questions in "tell me about the last time..." / "walk me
through what happened when..." form. Ensure follow-up probes dig into
concrete details (what tool, what step, what went wrong) rather than
general impressions.
```

## One-liner

```text
Continuous discovery: weekly customer touch, no exceptions; visible
opportunity solution tree with real breadth; interview for stories about
past events, not opinions; map assumptions, test the riskiest/least-
evidenced first; whole team participates, not just research.
```
