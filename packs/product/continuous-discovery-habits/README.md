# Pack: Continuous Discovery Habits

**Domain:** Product · **Authority:** Level 3 (methodology) · **Version:** 1.0.0

Operationalizes Teresa Torres's weekly-cadence discovery practice: continuous customer interviews, opportunity solution trees for structured ideation, and assumption testing — making discovery a habit embedded in every week rather than a big up-front phase.

Independent distillation; not affiliated with or endorsed by the source author. See [references.md](references.md).

## When to load

- Establishing or reviewing a team's ongoing (weekly) discovery cadence.
- Structuring how opportunities (customer needs/pain points) map to potential solutions.
- Designing assumption tests before committing to a solution.
- Reviewing whether "customer research" is continuous or a one-off event before a big launch.
