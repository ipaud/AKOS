# Heuristics — Continuous Discovery Habits Pack

- **The weekly-touch check:** did the team have a real customer conversation this week? If the honest answer is "not this week, we were busy building," the habit has already broken — protect the weekly slot like a recurring meeting.
- **Story-elicitation phrasing:** "tell me about the last time..." / "walk me through what happened when..." beats "would you..." / "do you think you'd..." — catch and rephrase hypothetical questions during interview prep.
- **Tree-breadth check:** does the opportunity solution tree show ≥2 opportunities under the outcome and ≥2 solutions under any opportunity being pursued? A tree with one opportunity and one solution is a decision already made, dressed as exploration.
- **Assumption-mapping shortcut:** for a candidate solution, ask "what would have to be true for this to fail?" — each answer is an assumption; the one that scares the team most and has the least evidence goes first.
- **Signal vs. noise in interviews:** one customer mentioning a pain point is a data point; three unconnected customers mentioning the same pain point unprompted is a pattern worth acting on.
- **Synthesis lag check:** how long between an interview and its findings landing on the opportunity tree? If it's weeks, synthesis has become a batch process again — fix by synthesizing within a day of each interview.
- **Cross-functional attendance check:** who's actually in the interview room (or reviewing the recording within the week)? If it's only a researcher or only a PM, engineering/design are working from secondhand summaries, not primary signal.
