# Engineering Rules — Continuous Discovery Habits Pack

Process/artifact rules (this pack governs discovery process).

- CDE1. At least one customer interview or equivalent direct-customer-contact activity occurs per team per week; gaps are tracked and flagged, not silently skipped.
- CDE2. An opportunity solution tree exists per team-owned outcome, updated within days of each new research data point (not batched).
- CDE3. Interview guides use story-eliciting questions about specific past events; hypothetical/opinion-soliciting questions are flagged in interview-prep review.
- CDE4. Before a solution is committed to build, its assumption map (desirability/usability/feasibility/viability) is documented, with the highest-importance/least-evidenced assumption identified as the test priority.
- CDE5. At least the smallest viable experiment is run against the top-priority assumption before full build commitment, with results recorded against the tree.
- CDE6. Interview attendance/synthesis participation includes at least one person each from product, design, and engineering on a rotating basis — not exclusively a research function.
