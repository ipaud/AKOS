# Principles — Continuous Discovery Habits Pack

- **CD1 — Touch customer reality every week**, without exception, even briefly (one interview, one usability test, one support-ticket review session) — consistency beats occasional depth.
- **CD2 — Maintain a visible opportunity solution tree** rooted in the team's owned outcome; update it continuously as research produces new opportunities and solution ideas.
- **CD3 — Interview for stories about specific past events**, not opinions about hypotheticals or reactions to concepts.
- **CD4 — Explore multiple opportunities before committing to one**, and multiple solutions per opportunity before committing to one — visible on the tree, not just in someone's head.
- **CD5 — Map assumptions explicitly before testing.** Identify desirability/usability/feasibility/viability/ethical assumptions for a candidate solution; prioritize testing by importance × lack of evidence.
- **CD6 — Test the riskiest, least-evidenced assumption first**, with the smallest experiment that produces a real answer.
- **CD7 — Synthesize continuously, not in a single end-of-study readout.** Update the opportunity tree and team understanding after every interview, not just after a batch is "done."
- **CD8 — Discovery habits are a team sport.** Product, design, and engineering participate in interviews and tree-building together — not a research function reporting findings to a separate build function.
