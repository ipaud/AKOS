# Engineering Rules — Lean Startup Pack

Process/artifact rules (this pack governs product process).

- LE1. Every new initiative under genuine market uncertainty has a written leap-of-faith assumption (value + growth hypothesis) before build starts.
- LE2. The MVP scope is justified against the specific hypothesis it tests — features not serving that test are cut from the MVP and deferred.
- LE3. Success metrics are defined before the experiment launches, distinguishing actionable metrics (cohort-based, causally attributable) from vanity metrics (cumulative, non-actionable) — vanity metrics may be tracked but are not the decision criterion.
- LE4. A baseline measurement using real data is recorded before any optimization experiment begins.
- LE5. A pivot-or-persevere checkpoint date/trigger is set in advance for any multi-experiment initiative, not decided ad hoc after the fact.
- LE6. When a pivot occurs, the invalidated assumption and the new hypothesis being tested are documented explicitly.
