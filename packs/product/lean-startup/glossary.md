# Glossary — Lean Startup Pack

- **Build-Measure-Learn** — the core iteration loop; entered by defining what to learn.
- **Validated learning** — rigorous, empirical proof that a hypothesis is true or false.
- **MVP (Minimum Viable Product)** — the smallest thing that generates validated learning about the riskiest assumption.
- **Leap-of-faith assumption** — a critical, unproven assumption the whole plan depends on (value/growth hypothesis).
- **Value hypothesis** — the assumption that customers will use/want/pay for the product.
- **Growth hypothesis** — the assumption about how the product will acquire customers at scale.
- **Innovation accounting** — measuring progress via baseline → tuning → pivot/persevere, instead of traditional accounting.
- **Vanity metric** — a metric that trends favorably regardless of real business impact.
- **Actionable metric** — a metric causally, repeatably movable by a specific team action.
- **Pivot** — a structured change to a fundamental hypothesis, informed by validated learning.
- **Concierge MVP / Wizard-of-Oz MVP** — manually-delivered or faked-automation MVPs testing demand before building the real thing.
