# Review Checklist — Lean Startup Pack

## High

- [ ] Leap-of-faith assumption (value + growth hypothesis) documented before build, for initiatives under genuine uncertainty. (LE1)
- [ ] MVP scope traces to the specific hypothesis it tests; extraneous features cut. (LE2)
- [ ] Success metric defined before launch, and it's actionable (cohort-based, causally attributable), not vanity. (LE3)
- [ ] Baseline measured with real data before optimization experiments begin. (LE4)

## Medium

- [ ] Pivot-or-persevere checkpoint scheduled in advance for multi-experiment initiatives. (LE5)
- [ ] Any pivot documents the invalidated assumption and the new hypothesis. (LE6)
- [ ] Loop cycle time (Build-Measure-Learn) is reasonable for the initiative's risk level — not stretched over months for a simple test.

## Low

- [ ] Vanity metrics, if displayed, are labeled as context rather than decision criteria.
