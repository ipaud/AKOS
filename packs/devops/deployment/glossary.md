# Glossary — Deployment Pack

- **Blast radius** — the scope of impact if a deployment introduces a problem.
- **Canary release** — progressive traffic rollout to a small subset before full deployment.
- **Blue-green deployment** — two full environments with atomic traffic switch, enabling instant rollback.
- **Rolling deployment** — gradually replacing instances of the old version with the new one.
- **Expand-contract** — a safe migration pattern: add new, migrate, then remove old, across separate deploys.
- **Smoke test** — a quick post-deploy check verifying critical functionality works.
