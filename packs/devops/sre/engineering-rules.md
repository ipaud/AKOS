# Engineering Rules — SRE Pack

- SR-E1. Production services have documented SLIs/SLOs for their critical user journeys.
- SR-E2. Paging alerts are symptom-based (SLO-threatening) and each has a documented "why this pages" rationale.
- SR-E3. Every alert has a linked runbook or clear next-step documentation.
- SR-E4. Postmortems are written for incidents above a defined severity threshold, blameless in tone, with follow-up actions tracked to completion.
- SR-E5. Error budget status is visible/reviewed on a regular cadence (e.g. weekly) by the team owning the service.
- SR-E6. Recurring manual operational tasks are tracked as toil and prioritized for automation.
