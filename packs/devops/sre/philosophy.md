# Philosophy — SRE Pack

Perfect reliability isn't the goal — it's usually not even desirable, because chasing 100% uptime trades away the velocity that makes a product worth using. The error budget reframes reliability as a resource to spend deliberately: define the acceptable failure rate (SLO) up front, and as long as you're within budget, ship features and take risks; once the budget is exhausted, shift focus to stability until it recovers. This turns "reliability vs. velocity" from a perpetual political argument into a number both sides already agreed to.
