# Glossary — SRE Pack

- **SLI (Service Level Indicator)** — a measured metric of service behavior (e.g. request latency).
- **SLO (Service Level Objective)** — a target value/range for an SLI.
- **SLA (Service Level Agreement)** — a customer-facing commitment, often with financial/contractual consequences.
- **Error budget** — the allowed amount of SLO violation before reliability work takes priority over features.
- **Toil** — repetitive, manual, automatable operational work.
- **Blameless postmortem** — an incident review focused on systemic causes, not individual fault.
- **Runbook** — documented step-by-step response procedure for a known failure mode.
- **On-call** — the rotation of engineers responsible for responding to production alerts.
