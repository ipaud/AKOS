# Philosophy — QA Checklists Pack

Automated tests verify what someone thought to write a test for; exploratory QA finds what nobody thought of — the empty state nobody designed, the network failure mid-submit, the double-click on a not-yet-disabled button. Checklists exist not to replace judgment but to make systematic what human attention tends to skip under deadline pressure: the boring states (empty/loading/error), the unglamorous edge cases (very long input, zero items, offline), and the unsexy cross-browser/device matrix that most manual testing naturally narrows away from.
