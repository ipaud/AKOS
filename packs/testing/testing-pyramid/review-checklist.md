# Review Checklist — Testing Pyramid Pack

## High
- [ ] New business logic has unit test coverage. (TY1)
- [ ] API/service boundaries have integration tests. (TY2)
- [ ] Flaky tests fixed or quarantined, not left intermittent. (TY4)

## Medium
- [ ] E2E tests limited to critical journeys, justified when added. (TY3)
- [ ] Suite shape (unit/integration/E2E ratio) roughly pyramid, not inverted.

## Low
- [ ] CI reports layer breakdown for visibility.
