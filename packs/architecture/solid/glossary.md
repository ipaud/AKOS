# Glossary — SOLID Pack

- **SRP (Single Responsibility Principle)** — one reason to change per class/module.
- **OCP (Open/Closed Principle)** — open for extension, closed for modification.
- **LSP (Liskov Substitution Principle)** — subtypes substitutable for their base type without breaking callers.
- **ISP (Interface Segregation Principle)** — clients depend only on methods they use.
- **DIP (Dependency Inversion Principle)** — high- and low-level modules depend on abstractions owned by the consumer.
- **Axis of change** — a distinct reason/stakeholder that would drive a class to change.
- **Cohesion** — how tightly a module's responsibilities belong together.
- **Coupling** — how much a module depends on another's concrete details.
- **Behavioral subtyping** — the LSP requirement that overriding preserve caller-visible contracts.
- **Refused bequest** — a subclass that overrides inherited behavior to reject it (LSP smell).
