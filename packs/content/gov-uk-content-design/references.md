# References — GOV.UK Content Design Pack

Attribution only. Nothing in this pack is copied from these sources; the pack is an original operational distillation. The originals are free to read and better on nuance — read them.

- **Content design: planning, writing and managing content** — Government Digital Service. https://www.gov.uk/guidance/content-design
- **Writing for GOV.UK** — Government Digital Service. https://www.gov.uk/guidance/content-design/writing-for-gov-uk
- **GOV.UK style guide** — Government Digital Service. https://www.gov.uk/guidance/style-guide
- **Government Design Principles** — Government Digital Service. https://www.gov.uk/guidance/government-design-principles
- **GOV.UK Service Manual** — Government Digital Service. https://www.gov.uk/service-manual
- **Government Digital Service blog** — Government Digital Service. https://gds.blog.gov.uk/
- **Design in government blog** — Government Digital Service. https://designnotes.blog.gov.uk/

## Adjacent reading

- **Content Design** — Sarah Winters (Sarah Richards), Content Design London. https://contentdesign.london/ — the book-length statement of the method that originated at GDS.
- **Making your service accessible: an introduction** — Government Digital Service. https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/helping-people-to-use-your-service — the assisted-digital and accessibility context this pack assumes.

GOV.UK guidance is published under an open government licence, which would permit broader reuse than this pack takes. AKOS policy is deliberately stricter: no reproduced sentences, no mirrored structure, no lifted examples. See [core/source-policy.md](../../../core/source-policy.md).
