# Glossary — Norman Pack

- **Affordance** — what actions an object actually permits an agent; exists independent of perception.
- **Signifier** — perceivable cue communicating an affordance; the designable half of the pair.
- **Mapping (natural)** — control-to-effect relationship exploiting spatial/logical analogy so effects are predictable without learning.
- **Feedback** — perceivable communication of action results and system state.
- **Conceptual model** — the user's internal explanation of how the system works; built solely from the system image.
- **System image** — everything perceivable about the product (UI, copy, docs, errors); the only channel to the user's model.
- **Constraint** — physical, semantic, cultural, or logical limitation that prevents wrong actions before they occur.
- **Forcing function** — hard constraint gating an action (interlock, lock-in, lockout); e.g. typed confirmation.
- **Gulf of execution** — the gap between intent and knowing what to do.
- **Gulf of evaluation** — the gap between acting and knowing what happened.
- **Seven stages of action** — goal, plan, specify, perform, perceive, interpret, compare; a diagnostic walk for failures.
- **Slip** — correct intention, faulty execution (attention failure).
- **Mistake** — faulty intention from a wrong model (understanding failure).
- **Mode error** — acting correctly for the wrong mode; the costliest mistake class; prevented by visible modes.
- **Knowledge in the world** — information carried by the environment (labels, visible options) rather than memory.
- **Visceral / behavioral / reflective** — three levels of emotional processing: immediate appearance, feel of use, story after use.
- **Human-centered design (HCD)** — iterative observe→prototype→test practice grounding design in observed behavior.
