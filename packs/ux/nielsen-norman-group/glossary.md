# Glossary — NN/g Pack

- **Heuristic evaluation** — systematic UI inspection against the ten heuristics by independent evaluators; findings merged and severity-rated.
- **Severity rating** — finding priority from frequency × impact × persistence; maps to AKOS CRITICAL/HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW.
- **Discount usability** — the doctrine that cheap frequent evaluation beats expensive rare evaluation, because the alternative in practice is none.
- **Evaluator effect** — different evaluators find different problem subsets; inspection is sampling.
- **Five-user rule** — ~5 users per iterative test round expose most discoverable problems in that design state.
- **Jakob's Law** — expectations form on everyone else's products; consistency imports that training for free.
- **Information scent** — predictive strength of navigation cues; weak scent causes pogo-sticking.
- **Pogo-sticking** — rapid click-into/back-out navigation signaling scent failure.
- **Progressive disclosure** — core visible by default, advanced revealed on request; resolves H7 vs H8.
- **Roach motel** — easy-in/hard-out pattern (H3 violation); dark pattern with legal exposure.
- **Expert cliff** — accelerators replacing the visible path instead of layering over it.
- **Learnability / efficiency / memorability / errors / satisfaction** — the five measurable attributes usability decomposes into.
