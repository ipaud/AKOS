# Pack: Universal Principles of Design

**Domain:** UX/Design theory · **Authority:** Level 3 (compiled design reference) · **Version:** 1.0.0

Operationalizes the cross-disciplinary design principles most useful in software that aren't already carried by other AKOS packs: Gestalt grouping, the 80/20 rule, progressive disclosure, chunking, confirmation, forgiveness, flexibility-usability tradeoff, performance load, and kin. This pack is the *connective tissue* — many entries here generalize what specific packs prescribe.

Independent distillation for personal engineering use. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the source authors. See [references.md](references.md).

## When to load

- Design theory questions falling between other packs' domains.
- Layout/grouping reviews (Gestalt) and information architecture calls.
- Feature prioritization arguments (80/20) and complexity budgeting.
- Teaching moments — this pack explains *why* the other packs agree with each other.
