# Anti-Patterns — Universal Principles Pack

## Gestalt lies

Layouts whose grouping cues misreport relationships: equal spacing across a semantic boundary, one card around two unrelated widgets, matching styles on functionally different buttons. Users "misread" — the layout lied. Fix: UD1–UD3.

## The everything-tool

Product accommodating every workflow until it serves none well (UP16). Detection: onboarding requires choosing among modes; docs need a persona picker; every feature has an exception toggle. Fix: focused core + priced flexibility (UD10); adjacent products beat one omniproduct.

## Democracy of features

Every feature equal nav billing regardless of usage — the 80/20 inversion. The five-times-a-day action shares a menu with the once-a-year export. Fix: UD6, promote by usage; disclose the tail.

## Punishing system

No undo anywhere; deletes are instant and final; "are you sure?" as the only safety; drafts don't exist. Users move timidly, adopt shallowly, and back up externally. Fix: UD9 forgiveness inventory — including at the storage layer (soft deletes, history).

## Confirmation-as-forgiveness

Believing dialogs make destruction safe (they make it *ritual* — [confirmation wallpaper](../don-norman/anti-patterns.md)). Fix: forgiveness mechanisms first; confirmation for the irreversible remainder.

## Noise as personality

Decorations, mascots, gradients, and micro-copy jokes on task-critical surfaces, defended as brand (UP11 violation wearing an identity costume). Personality belongs in the system's voice and visual tokens, not in extra elements ([L0 rule](../../personal/pau-avila/design-language.md): identity never at obviousness's expense).

## Settings landfill

Every design disagreement resolved by adding a toggle. Each toggle is deferred design + permanent flexibility mortgage (UP16, UD10). Fix: decide; measure; remove sub-2% toggles yearly.

## Open feedback loops

Actions with invisible delayed consequences: quota consumed silently, emails sent without confirmation trail, background jobs with no status surface. Users learn superstition instead of the system (UP15). Fix: UD12; consequences surface where users look.
