# Glossary — Universal Principles Pack

- **Gestalt principles** — pre-conscious perceptual grouping rules: proximity, similarity, continuity, closure, common region, figure-ground.
- **Common region** — grouping by enclosure; strongest and noisiest cue.
- **80/20 (Pareto) rule** — most value flows through a minority of features/actions.
- **Progressive disclosure** — staged revelation of complexity on demand.
- **Chunking** — restructuring items into meaningful groups to fit cognition.
- **Performance load** — total physical + cognitive cost of a task; the currency all efficiency principles reduce.
- **Forgiveness** — design that assumes error and makes recovery cheap (undo, drafts, retention).
- **Constraint** — preventing invalid action rather than warning about it.
- **Flexibility-usability tradeoff** — accommodation breadth degrades per-task usability.
- **Flexibility pricing** — recording who a new option serves and what it costs everyone else.
- **Signal-to-noise ratio** — information per element; decoration without signal is noise.
- **Feedback loop** — consequences of actions made visible where users act.
- **Iteration principle** — quality tracks iteration count; cheapen the loop.
