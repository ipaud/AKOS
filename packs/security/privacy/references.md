# References — Privacy Pack

Attribution only. Nothing here is copied from these documents; the pack is an original
operational distillation. The normative text is always the authority — where a rule in
this pack disagrees with the regulation or a regulator's guidance, they win.

**This pack is engineering guidance, not legal advice.** It helps you build a system that
can comply; it does not tell you whether you do.

## Primary sources (Level 1 — law and supervisory authorities)

- **Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation)** — European Union. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
- **Guidelines 05/2020 on consent under Regulation 2016/679** — European Data Protection Board. https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-052020-consent-under-regulation-2016679_en
- **Guidelines 4/2019 on Article 25 Data Protection by Design and by Default** — European Data Protection Board. https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/our-documents/guidelines/guidelines-42019-article-25-data-protection-design-and_en
- **Guía sobre el uso de las cookies** — Agencia Española de Protección de Datos. https://www.aepd.es/documento/guia-cookies.pdf

The AEPD guidance is included because it is the binding interpretation where this system's
owner operates; a project elsewhere should substitute its own supervisory authority's
equivalent and re-check the consent rules against it.

## Scope note

This pack takes an EU baseline. It does not restate other jurisdictions, does not cover
sector-specific regimes, and does not adjudicate whether a given processing is lawful.
