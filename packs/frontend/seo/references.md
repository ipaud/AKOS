# References — SEO Pack

Attribution only. Nothing here is copied from these sources; the pack is an original
operational distillation, organized by the AKOS file contract rather than by any source's
structure.

## Primary sources (Level 1 — platform owner and standards bodies)

- **Google Search Essentials** — Google. https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials
- **Google Search Central Documentation** — Google. https://developers.google.com/search/docs

## What Level 1 covers here, and what it does not

The level applies to **documented, enforceable mechanisms**: crawl directives, indexing
controls, canonicalization, structured-data eligibility requirements, and the spam
policies that carry actual penalties. Google is the platform owner for appearing in Google
Search, the same basis on which
[performance/web-dev](../../performance/web-dev/README.md),
[ux/material-design](../../ux/material-design/README.md), and
[ux/apple-hig](../../ux/apple-hig/README.md) are Level 1. RFC 9309 is a genuine IETF RFC.

It does **not** extend to ranking. Nothing about how results are ordered is published, so
nothing about it is in this pack at any level — not demoted to Level 4, excluded. This is
the domain with the largest gap between the volume of available advice and the volume of
verifiable advice, and the pack's usefulness depends on keeping that line visible.

A consequence worth stating: a source claiming to know ranking factors is Level 4 by
definition, and [source-policy](../../../core/source-policy.md) bars a Level 4 source from
being a pack's basis. There is no version of this pack that includes that material
honestly.
