# Philosophy — Design Systems Pack

A design system's job is to make the consistent choice the *easy* choice — every ad-hoc value a developer picks instead of a token is a design decision made by someone without full context, at the worst possible time (mid-implementation, under deadline pressure). The system pays off precisely to the degree developers reach for it by default rather than working around it; a system nobody uses because it's slower than hardcoding is a failed system regardless of its internal quality.
