# Product Knowledge Graph

How the four product packs relate. Parent: [knowledge-graph](knowledge-graph.md).

## The packs

- **Team/organization model:** [Inspired](../packs/product/inspired/README.md) (empowered teams, four risks), [Escaping the Build Trap](../packs/product/escaping-the-build-trap/README.md) (org-level anti-feature-factory).
- **Validation engine:** [Lean Startup](../packs/product/lean-startup/README.md) (Build-Measure-Learn, MVP, pivot).
- **Weekly practice:** [Continuous Discovery Habits](../packs/product/continuous-discovery-habits/README.md) (interviewing, opportunity trees, assumption mapping).

## The shared spine: outcomes over output

All four converge on the [outcomes-over-output node](knowledge-graph.md): measure whether behavior changed, not whether features shipped. Vocabularies differ by context —

- **0-to-1 / startup:** Lean Startup (MVP, validated learning, pivot).
- **Established product org:** Inspired (four risks, empowered teams).
- **Organizational dysfunction diagnosis:** Escaping the Build Trap (strategy vs backlog, cascade intent).
- **Ongoing team habit:** Continuous Discovery (weekly interviews, opportunity solution tree).

## Key concept edges

- **Four risks** (value/usability/feasibility/viability) — shared vocabulary across [Inspired](../packs/product/inspired/mental-models.md) and [Continuous Discovery assumption mapping](../packs/product/continuous-discovery-habits/mental-models.md).
- **MVP** ([Lean Startup](../packs/product/lean-startup/mental-models.md)) ↔ **prototype fidelity** ([Inspired](../packs/product/inspired/mental-models.md)) — smallest thing that generates validated learning.
- **Product kata iterate-step** ([Build Trap](../packs/product/escaping-the-build-trap/mental-models.md)) ↔ **Build-Measure-Learn** ([Lean Startup](../packs/product/lean-startup/mental-models.md)) ↔ **weekly discovery** ([Continuous Discovery](../packs/product/continuous-discovery-habits/principles.md)) — the same loop at three cadences.
- **Flexibility-usability tradeoff** ([Universal Principles](../packs/ux/universal-principles-of-design/principles.md)) ↔ **feature-factory / say-no** ([Build Trap](../packs/product/escaping-the-build-trap/principles.md)) — restraint at UX and product scale.

## Authority & posture

All L3 (methodologies). Product findings are **advisory** — the human owns product calls ([Ruling R10](../core/conflict-resolution.md), [product-reviewer](../agents/product-reviewer.md)). Scaled hard by profile: prototypes aren't held to full discovery rigor.
