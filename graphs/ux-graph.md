# UX Knowledge Graph

How the nine UX packs relate. Parent: [knowledge-graph](knowledge-graph.md).

## The layers

- **Practice (what to do):** [Krug](../packs/ux/steve-krug/README.md) (web usability habit), [NN/g](../packs/ux/nielsen-norman-group/README.md) (heuristic evaluation system).
- **Theory (why it works):** [Norman](../packs/ux/don-norman/README.md) (interaction psychology), [Laws of UX](../packs/ux/laws-of-ux/README.md) (quantified cognitive laws), [Universal Principles](../packs/ux/universal-principles-of-design/README.md) (cross-domain connective tissue).
- **Craft (how to make it look designed):** [Refactoring UI](../packs/ux/refactoring-ui/README.md).
- **Platform (the environment's grammar):** [Apple HIG](../packs/ux/apple-hig/README.md), [Material Design](../packs/ux/material-design/README.md).
- **Floor (non-negotiable):** [WCAG](../packs/ux/wcag/README.md).

## Key concept edges

- **Satisficing** ([Krug](../packs/ux/steve-krug/mental-models.md)) ↔ **Hick's Law under time pressure** ([Laws of UX](../packs/ux/laws-of-ux/principles.md)) — craft rule ↔ quantified mechanism.
- **Conventions** ([Krug P5](../packs/ux/steve-krug/principles.md)) ↔ **Jakob's Law** ([Laws of UX](../packs/ux/laws-of-ux/principles.md)) ↔ **Consistency H4** ([NN/g](../packs/ux/nielsen-norman-group/principles.md)) ↔ **platform grammar** ([HIG](../packs/ux/apple-hig/principles.md)/[Material](../packs/ux/material-design/principles.md)).
- **De-emphasize to emphasize** ([Refactoring UI RP2](../packs/ux/refactoring-ui/principles.md)) ↔ **Von Restorff** ([Laws of UX](../packs/ux/laws-of-ux/principles.md)) ↔ **one dominant action** ([Krug ER12](../packs/ux/steve-krug/engineering-rules.md)).
- **Error recovery** ([NN/g H9](../packs/ux/nielsen-norman-group/principles.md)) ↔ **slips vs mistakes** ([Norman](../packs/ux/don-norman/mental-models.md)) ↔ **forgiveness** ([Universal](../packs/ux/universal-principles-of-design/principles.md)) ↔ **error identification** ([WCAG](../packs/ux/wcag/principles.md)).
- **Proximity/grouping** ([Universal Gestalt](../packs/ux/universal-principles-of-design/principles.md)) ↔ **spacing groups** ([Refactoring UI RP8](../packs/ux/refactoring-ui/principles.md)) ↔ **related items together** ([Krug ER13](../packs/ux/steve-krug/engineering-rules.md)).

## Authority ordering within UX

WCAG (L1) > platform guidelines on their platform (L1 scoped) > NN/g, Krug, Norman (L2) > Refactoring UI, Laws of UX, Universal Principles (L3). Personal [design-language](../packs/personal/pau-avila/design-language.md) (L0) overrides aesthetic choices but never the floor ([Ruling R1/R4/R5](../core/conflict-resolution.md)).
